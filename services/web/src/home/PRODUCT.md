# Secure Critical Infrastructure homepage

## Register
brand

## Platform
web

## Purpose and audience
Public home for Isaac (Nanzheng)'s research-led cyber and AI assurance work. The user requests a consulting-style React homepage with Mission, About and a See demo link. It introduces the work to critical-infrastructure operators, research collaborators and fellowship audiences already identified in the project proposals.

## Positioning
Keep humans in control as AI enters critical infrastructure. Local AI deployment, independent authority gates, contextual evidence and cross-border technical collaboration are the research themes. The hosted simulator is an internet-connected research sandbox; it is not evidence of real-plant safety or offline deployment.

## Conversion and proof
Primary action: See demo at https://ot-aigent-simulation.night-zone.com. Secondary actions: read recorded evidence and explore the public GitHub project. Proof: the actual open-source lab and its production verification. No invented clients, testimonials, certifications, partnerships or improvement claims. About uses the user's supplied PUB cybersecurity background and M3 fellowship context. Meetings with organisations do not imply endorsement.

## Voice and direction
Practical, assured, inquisitive. LFG Content Co is the user-selected reference for strong typography, section pacing and responsive hover treatments. Use concise copy and distinctive motion; avoid generic AI fonts and feature-explanation clutter. The registered domain is securecritcticalinfra.dev (Cloudflare verified).

## Implementation judgement
A separate public brand entry in the existing React/Vite project, deployed in a separate static Worker. Bright-room laptop and mobile viewing favour white surfaces, strong green identity and high-contrast text. Accessible keyboard interaction and reduced-motion fallbacks are implementation defaults.
