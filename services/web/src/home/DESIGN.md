# Visual direction

Single deliberately chosen Archivo variable family: typeset like a public infrastructure identity, with heavy headlines and restrained body text. Reflex choices Inter, Space Grotesk and IBM Plex were rejected for this new brand surface. Reference catalogue: Google Fonts Archivo.

Committed green (seed hue 150), pure white, pale green structural surfaces and dark green ink. Physical scene: an infrastructure leader looking at the site on a laptop in a bright conference foyer, moving from an introduction to a demonstrable research tool.

Large split hero with an interactive, isometric infrastructure drawing; sector buttons select the scene and its concern. A full-green mission section supplies visual contrast. The approach unfolds as expandable rows, followed by an actual simulator screenshot and research scope, then the founder/field-building story. CTA arrows travel on hover; scene highlights connect selection to infrastructure. No cursor replacement or scroll hijacking. All content visible without entrance-animation classes. Reduced motion disables spatial animation.

Spacing follows 4/8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96px, with fluid outer gutters. Display type max 6rem, heading tracking no tighter than -.04em. No repeated eyebrow scaffold, icon-card grid, decorative gradients or fabricated metrics.
